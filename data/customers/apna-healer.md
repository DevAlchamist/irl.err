## The problem

Apna Healer had a growing operational challenge. Managing clients, therapist availability, appointment bookings, communication, and event coordination across different processes was creating unnecessary bottlenecks.

Slot allocation was particularly troublesome. Clients needed a smoother way to find and book available sessions, while therapists and team members needed a reliable system to manage their schedules and payments.

The organization needed more than a website. It needed a central platform that could bring its entire operation together.

## What changed

IRL.err started from scratch and developed a complete digital ecosystem for Apna Healer, bringing its website, application, booking workflows, and operational tools under one roof.

The platform included:

- A complete website and application built around Apna Healer's requirements
- An appointment booking system with automated slot allocation
- Therapist and emotional support matchmaking to connect people with appropriate support
- Automated messaging and email communication
- An event management platform integrated into the main ecosystem
- A dashboard for managing clients, appointments, and operational activities
- Commission-based payment calculations to simplify compensation and reduce manual effort
- Marketing solutions to support the platform's outreach and growth

The goal was to make the experience easier for everyone involved: clients booking sessions, therapists managing their availability, and administrators handling day-to-day operations.

## The outcome

Within the first week of using the improved workflows, the team began seeing the difference.

Clients could navigate the booking process more easily and receive their allotted time slots. Therapists and employees had clearer schedules and more reliable payment calculations. The commission-based payment system also reduced the amount of manual work required from the organization.

After approximately two months of development, including changes based on the client's feedback, the complete platform was handed over to Apna Healer.

The result was a unified system designed to replace fragmented processes with a more organized, automated way of operating.
