## The problem

Xploroo needed a digital platform that could serve two different purposes: connecting people with study-abroad consultation services and helping users discover and rent properties.

Both services required different workflows. Property owners needed a way to list and manage their properties, vendors needed their own management interface, and customers needed an easy way to explore availability and make bookings.

Managing these separate requirements through disconnected systems would make the experience unnecessarily complicated.

## What changed

IRL.err developed a dual-purpose platform that brings study consultation and property rentals together in one digital ecosystem.

The platform includes:

- A study consultation system for people exploring opportunities to study abroad
- A property rental marketplace with property discovery and listings
- A dedicated landlord portal to list and manage rental properties
- A vendor portal for managing property-related operations
- Property availability and time-based booking management
- A streamlined booking experience allowing users to select properties according to their requirements and preferred dates

The focus was to give every type of user a dedicated experience while keeping the entire ecosystem connected through one platform.

## The outcome

Xploroo received a unified digital platform covering both study consultation and property rentals.

Landlords and vendors have dedicated portals to manage their respective activities, while customers can explore services, browse available properties, and make bookings based on their preferred timing.

Instead of building two disconnected products, the project brought both business models together into one organized digital experience.
