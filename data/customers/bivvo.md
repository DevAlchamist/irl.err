## The problem

Managing events involves more than just creating an event page. Organizers need to manage participants, coordinate volunteers, handle registrations, and keep everyone informed throughout the process.

Bivvo needed a centralized platform where organizers, volunteers, and attendees could interact with the same event ecosystem while having different experiences and capabilities.

The challenge was to create a structured system that could handle these different user roles, simplify onboarding, and make event management easier from creation to participation.

## What changed

IRL.err developed Bivvo as a dedicated event and volunteer coordination platform, with separate experiences for organizers, volunteers, and attendees.

The platform included:

- Role-based authentication and onboarding for organizers, volunteers, and attendees
- Email and password authentication alongside Google Sign-In
- Dedicated user profiles based on individual roles
- Profile management with image uploads
- Event creation and management functionality
- Draft and publish workflows for organizers
- A centralized event management system
- A structured foundation for volunteer applications and coordination

The platform was designed around a clear principle: every user should have the tools they need without being overwhelmed by features meant for someone else.

## The outcome

Bivvo gained a unified digital foundation for managing events and bringing organizers, volunteers, and attendees together.

Organizers could create and publish events, users could onboard according to their roles, and individual profiles provided a structured way to manage participation across the platform.

Instead of treating event management and volunteer coordination as separate processes, Bivvo brought them into one connected ecosystem designed to support future expansion.
