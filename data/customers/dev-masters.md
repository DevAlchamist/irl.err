## The problem

Dev Masters required software solutions across several distinct industries, each with its own workflows, business logic, and operational requirements.

From managing hospital operations to building specialized trading and betting systems, these projects required more than standard website development. Each platform needed functionality tailored to its particular business model and user experience.

The challenge was to translate these different requirements into working digital products without treating every project as the same kind of application.

## What changed

IRL.err worked on multiple platforms for Dev Masters, covering healthcare management, financial trading, and specialized betting systems.

The development work included:

- A hospital management system to support healthcare-related operations and workflows
- Forex trading platform development
- Diamond betting platform development
- Betfair-related betting platform functionality
- Cryptocurrency-based betting systems
- Multiple specialized betting workflows and platform features
- Custom functionality developed around the requirements of each platform

Each project involved its own set of requirements, business logic, and technical considerations, with development focused on making the intended workflows functional and accessible.

## The outcome

Dev Masters received development support across a diverse portfolio of digital platforms, extending from healthcare management to specialized trading and betting applications.

Rather than relying on a single generic solution, each platform was developed around its respective operational requirements, giving the business a collection of purpose-built digital products across different domains.
