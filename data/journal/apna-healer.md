Building a mental wellness platform involves more than putting therapists and clients in one place. Behind every appointment are schedules to manage, availability to track, payments to calculate, and people who need timely support.

That was the challenge behind **Apna Healer**. The goal was to build a complete platform from scratch that could make the experience smoother for clients while reducing the operational work involved in running the service.

**## Bringing the experience together**

We built a platform that connected clients with therapists through a matchmaking experience, making it easier for people to find the support they needed.

Appointment booking, automated slot allocation, messaging, and email notifications were brought into one workflow. Clients could book appointments without relying on constant manual coordination, while the team could manage schedules and availability more efficiently.

We also integrated event management to support activities beyond individual appointments.

**## Fixing the work behind the scenes**

A good booking experience is only one part of the system. The operational side needed just as much attention.

We implemented commission-based payment management, along with calculations for employee payments and allocated schedules. This helped reduce the need for manually tracking commissions, coordinating appointment slots, and working out payment details.

The focus was simple: let the platform handle repetitive coordination so the team could spend more time focusing on people.

**## What changed**

After the improved workflows were introduced, the team started seeing practical improvements. Clients experienced fewer appointment-slot issues, employees received more accurate schedule allocations and payment calculations, and the team had less manual commission-related work to handle.

The platform was handed over after approximately two months of development, following iterations and client feedback. The improvements were focused on making everyday operations more organized and manageable.

**## The idea we built around**

Technology should make important services easier to access, not add another layer of complexity.

Apna Healer brought client matchmaking, appointment management, communication, event management, and payment workflows into one connected platform.

Because when the work behind a service becomes simpler, people can focus more on the service itself.
